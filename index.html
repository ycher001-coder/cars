Safety and Comfort Improvements: Over the 20th century, features like turn signals (1939), power steering (1951), three-point seatbelts (1959), and airbags (1973) became standard. Technological Shift: The late 20th century to present has shifted toward convenience and sustainability, introducing electronic fuel injection, hybrid systems (1997), and smart, electric vehicles (2012)


Cars have evolved from 19th-century steam and gasoline "horseless carriages"—like the 1886 Benz Patent-Motorwagen—into sophisticated, high-tech machines. The introduction of the assembly line by Henry Ford in 1908 made them accessible, transitioning from luxury items to necessities. Key advancements, including safer bodies, airbags, and automatic transmission, have dramatically improved safety and convenience, culminating in today's electric, connected, and increasingly automated vehicles.
